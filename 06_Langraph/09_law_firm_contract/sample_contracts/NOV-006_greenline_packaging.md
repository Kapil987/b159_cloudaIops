# PACKAGING SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Contract ID: NOV-006

## 1. Parties
Buyer: Nova Foods Ltd ("Nova"). Supplier: GreenLine Packaging Co ("GreenLine").
GreenLine supplies cartons and film for Nova's products.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This agreement starts on 6 February 2025 and expires on 5 February 2027.
It renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 45 days before expiry.

## 3. Payment
Nova pays about USD 480,000 per year. Invoices are due within 30 days.

## 4. Termination
Nova may terminate if GreenLine misses two delivery dates in any 3-month period.

## 5. Confidentiality
Product designs and print artwork are confidential.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
The Supplier's liability for any breach is not capped and extends to all direct and indirect losses suffered by the Buyer.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
