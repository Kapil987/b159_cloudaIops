# PAYMENT PROCESSING AGREEMENT
Contract ID: NOV-005

## 1. Parties
Merchant: Nova Foods Ltd ("Nova"). Processor: Orbit Payments Inc ("Orbit").
Orbit processes card payments from Nova's online store.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This agreement starts on 1 July 2025 and expires on 30 June 2028.
It renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 90 days before expiry.

## 3. Fees
Nova pays Orbit about USD 90,000 per year in processing fees.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach that is not fixed within 30 days of written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Cardholder data must be handled under applicable payment security rules.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Orbit's total liability is limited to the greater of the fees paid in the last 24 months or USD 500,000.
This cap does not apply to data breaches or fraud. For those, no limitation applies.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
