# PRODUCT SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Contract ID: NOV-003

## 1. Parties
Seller: Nova Foods Ltd ("Nova"). Buyer: BrightMart Retail Ltd ("BrightMart").
Nova supplies packaged food products to BrightMart's stores.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This agreement starts on 16 February 2024 and expires on 15 February 2027.
It renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 30 days before expiry.

## 3. Payment
BrightMart pays Nova about USD 1,200,000 per year. Invoices are due within 45 days.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach that is not fixed within 30 days of written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Pricing and volume forecasts are confidential.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
The Seller shall be liable for all losses arising from its breach of this Agreement.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
