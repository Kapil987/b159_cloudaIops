# DELIVERY SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract ID: NOV-004

## 1. Parties
Customer: Nova Foods Ltd ("Nova"). Carrier: Quickship Couriers LLP ("Quickship").
Quickship delivers Nova's urgent orders within the city.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This agreement starts on 21 January 2025 and expires on 20 January 2027.
This agreement does not renew. Any extension needs a new written agreement signed by both parties.

## 3. Payment
Nova pays about USD 150,000 per year, billed monthly based on deliveries made.

## 4. Termination
Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Delivery addresses and customer lists are confidential.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Neither party's liability under this Agreement is subject to any cap or ceiling.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
