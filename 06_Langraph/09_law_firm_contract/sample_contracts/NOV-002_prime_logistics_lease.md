# WAREHOUSE LEASE AGREEMENT
Contract ID: NOV-002

## 1. Parties
Tenant: Nova Foods Ltd ("Nova"). Landlord: Prime Logistics Parks Pvt Ltd ("Prime").
Prime leases cold-storage warehouse space to Nova.

## 2. Term and Renewal
The lease starts on 1 March 2024 and expires on 28 February 2027.
The lease renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before expiry.

## 3. Rent
Nova pays rent of USD 360,000 per year, in equal monthly instalments on the first day of each month.

## 4. Termination
Nova may end the lease early only by paying the remaining rent for the next 3 months.

## 5. Confidentiality
The financial terms of this lease are confidential.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Prime's liability to Nova for any claim is limited to an amount equal to three (3) months of rent.

## 7. Governing Law
This lease is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
