# SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract ID: NOV-001

## 1. Parties
Customer: Nova Foods Ltd ("Nova"). Provider: CloudServe Inc ("CloudServe").
CloudServe provides cloud inventory software to Nova's warehouses.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This agreement starts on 1 January 2025 (the Effective Date) and runs for three (3) years, expiring on 31 December 2027.
This agreement renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 90 days before expiry.

## 3. Payment
Nova pays CloudServe USD 200,000 per year, invoiced in advance on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not fixed within 30 days of written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's non-public information secret during the term and for 5 years after.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
CloudServe's total liability is limited to the fees Nova paid in the preceding 12 months.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
