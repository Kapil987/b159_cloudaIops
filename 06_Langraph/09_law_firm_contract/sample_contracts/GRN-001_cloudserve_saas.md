# SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract ID: GRN-001

## 1. Parties
Customer: Greenfield Retail Inc ("Greenfield"). Provider: CloudServe Inc ("CloudServe").
CloudServe provides point-of-sale software to Greenfield's shops.

## 2. Term and Renewal
This agreement starts on 1 June 2025 and expires on 31 May 2027.
It renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before expiry.

## 3. Payment
Greenfield pays CloudServe USD 50,000 per year.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach that is not fixed within 15 days of written notice.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's non-public information secret.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
CloudServe's total liability is capped at a flat USD 50,000, whatever the cause.

## 7. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
