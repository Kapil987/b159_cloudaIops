# SHOP LEASE AGREEMENT
Contract ID: GRN-002

## 1. Parties
Tenant: Greenfield Retail Inc ("Greenfield"). Landlord: Metro Realty Partners ("Metro").
Metro leases a retail shop in the city centre to Greenfield.

## 2. Term and Renewal
The lease starts on 11 January 2024 and expires on 10 January 2027.
The lease renews automatically for 1 year unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before expiry.

## 3. Rent
Greenfield pays rent of USD 120,000 per year, monthly in advance.

## 4. Termination
Greenfield may end the lease early with 6 months' notice and a fee of 2 months' rent.

## 5. Confidentiality
The financial terms of this lease are confidential.

## 6. Limitation of Liability
Metro's liability to Greenfield is limited to 12 months of rent.

## 7. Governing Law
This lease is governed by the laws of the State of California.
